# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Diners, restaurant visitors, and retail customers browsing products on mobile phones and desktops; business owners managing catalogs and receiving WhatsApp orders.

## Product Purpose

NEGOVA Digital Menu turns live inventory into a high-conversion, interactive digital menu and catalog. It empowers customers to browse categories, customize product variants and modifiers, and place orders directly via WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Zero-friction, interactive web-native menu experience that replaces static PDFs and printed sheets with a high-performance web app directly synchronized with NEGOVA POS inventory and business details.

## Operating Context

Quick service restaurants, casual dining, cafes, bars, and retail stores. Customers typically access via QR code scans at tables or links in Instagram/WhatsApp bios on mobile devices under varying lighting conditions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 4 distinctive templates: Comercial (clean catalog), Urbana Fast Food (dynamic, energetic, street-smart), Elegante & Clásica (fine dining, luxury editorial), Visual (visual showcase, photography-forward).
- Real inventory integration: dynamic categories, variants (sizes/options with price deltas), modifier groups (single/multi selection), allergens, preparation time.
- Integrated interactive Product Detail Modal for instant customization and WhatsApp order generation.
- Strict constraint: NO native alerts (`window.alert`, `window.confirm`, `window.prompt`). Custom modals and toasts only.
- Strict constraint: Never invent placeholder data or fake products; use live business data.

## Product Principles

1. **Native Web Fluidity**: Not a paper flyer or PDF replica. Responsive, touch-friendly, sticky category navigation, fast search, and smooth bottom-sheet modals.
2. **Honest Craftsmanship**: Strict adherence to typography scales, contrast ratios (≥4.5:1), purposeful elevation, and eliminating AI clichés (no gradient text, no eyebrows/kickers, no nested cards).
3. **Conversion-Driven Simplicity**: Transparent pricing, prominent call-to-actions, clear variant selection, and direct WhatsApp dispatch.
